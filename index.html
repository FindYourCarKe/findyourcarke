How it works

Buying a car

  1. Find a car you like. Each listing shows the price, mileage and condition notes.
  2. Ask about it on WhatsApp. Quote the car's reference from its yellow tag, for example CAR-00001.
  3. View it in person. We arrange the viewing with the owner.

Selling your car

  1. Message us about your car. Tell us the make, model, year and your asking price.
  2. We check the paperwork. We match your logbook to your ID before anything goes live.
  3. We list it and handle enquiries. Buyers contact us, so your number stays private.
  4. You pay only if it sells. A fixed fee, agreed in writing before we list your car.

Selling a car in Nairobi?

Send us the details on WhatsApp. We'll reply with what we need to list it.
